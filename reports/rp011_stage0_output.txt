=== WHY THE OPENING BLOCK IS NEARLY EMPTY -- distributions, not outcomes ===
  The aggression gate is a percentile POOLED over the whole session.
  Opening windows carry the most volume and the LEAST one-sidedness,
  so they almost never clear a pooled p90 imbalance.

  This is RP-008's pooled-tercile defect in a new place: a threshold
  pooled across the session partly encodes TIME OF DAY, which is the
  very axis RP-011 sets out to stratify on.
