Level Touch Scorecard

Price has reached one of your five fixed levels. Before the 5-minute bar closes, decide whether it holds or goes through — then find out if you were right. Four reads, each worth one point. The sum is your call.

  • 01Price reaches a level — pause the replay
  • 02Score the four reads below, −1 to +1 each
  • 03Write the total before resuming
  • 04Resume. Log what actually happened

The four reads

01

Depth of Market

Is there resting size sitting at the level, and does it survive the approach?

+1Large resting order at the level that gets hit and reappears. Someone is defending it.
−1Thin, or size pulled as price approached. Nobody is there.
02

Cluster at the level

Does the footprint cell at that price swell while price stops advancing?

+1Cell grows bar over bar at one price while the high or low stops extending.
−1Cell stays small and price walks through it. Thin means undefended.
03

Delta against price

The whole game in one question: is the aggression achieving anything?

+1Delta pushes hard, price doesn't move. They're paying the spread and getting nowhere.
−1Delta and price move together. The aggressor is being paid — expect it to continue.
04

Speed of tape

What happens to the rate of trades in the seconds after price arrives?

+1Spikes, then dies. The push spent itself at the level.
−1Accelerating through. Participation is building, not fading.
Any read you genuinely can't see scores 0. Guessing to fill the box is how you end up measuring your imagination instead of the tape.

Log the number, not just the word. At the end you compare your hit rate at |score| ≥ 3 against your hit rate at |score| = 2. If accuracy climbs as conviction climbs, the reads are carrying real information. If a 4 is no better than a 2, you are pattern-matching noise — and that is the single most useful thing this exercise can tell you.

Decide before you start — 20–30 sessions, 80–120 touches

Hit rateWhat it meansWhat you do

≤ 54% Tick data adds nothing here Stop. You've closed the question yourself.

Call it before the bar closes, every single time. The moment you allow yourself to wait for the close, you have reproduced the backtest — and that answer is already known: flat at every stop width, both halves of two years.

Written against 523 sessions of testing in which fading these levels blind lost −0.513R at t = −10.30, and requiring the bar to close back through recovered 0.47R of it. The read is right. This is the test of whether you can act on it in time.
