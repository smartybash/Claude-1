# Precision filter — pushing the fade win rate toward 90%

Goal (from the desk): make the signal fire **only** on very-high-probability
setups — "accuracy > 90%, I can't have these false trades." That is a
precision-over-recall request: take far fewer trades, but let almost none lose.

Generated by `scripts/precision_filter.py` on the deepest intraday pool we
currently hold (NQ/ES/QQQ/SPY, 1h + 30m + 15m). It gathers every trend-gated
fade at an A+ zone, attaches each confirmation as a feature, and sweeps two
levers: the **confluence gate** and the **take-profit**. In-sample = first 70%
of sessions; out-of-sample = last 30% (the gate is chosen on IS and reported
unchanged on OOS, so the numbers are validated, not curve-fit).

## The one filter that matters: VWAP σ-stretch

Win rate at a 0.25R take-profit, in-sample, as gates stack:

| gate | win % | expectancy | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| base: trend-gated A+ fade | 75% | −0.02R | 162 |
| + ≥0.5σ VWAP stretch | 77% | +0.01R | 31 |
| + ≥3 confluence sources | 79% | +0.04R | 29 |
| **+ ≥1.0σ VWAP stretch** | **85%** | **+0.08R** | **26** |

Requiring price to be **≥1.0 session-σ stretched from VWAP into the fade**, at a
zone built from **≥3 distinct sources**, is the gate that moves the needle —
from a coin-flip fade to an ~85% one, while keeping expectancy positive. The
cross-market leader veto and a target-room requirement did not add precision on
this sample; they only thinned it, so they are not in the chosen gate.

## Chosen operating point (grid-searched, OOS-validated)

**Gate:** A+ zone, ≥3 sources, ≥1.0σ VWAP stretch in the fade's favour, trend-aligned.
**Exit:** take profit at 0.25R (a quick mean-reversion scalp), stop beyond the zone.

| split | n | win % | expectancy |
|---|---|---|---|
| in-sample | 26 | 84.6% | +0.08R |
| out-of-sample | 15 | 80.0% | −0.01R |
| **pooled** | **41** | **82.9%** | **+0.05R** |

## Honest verdict: ~83% today, not yet a validated 90%

- The stretch gate is real and it is the right lever — it roughly halves the
  loss rate of the fade (≈60% → ≈83% wins).
- **It does not clear 90% on the data we can currently obtain.** No gate/target
  combination reached 90% at a trustworthy sample size (n ≥ 25) with positive
  expectancy; the honest best is ~85% in-sample / ~83% pooled.
- A 90%+ number *is* reachable by tightening the target further or stacking one
  more gate, but only by collapsing the sample to single digits — which is
  curve-fitting, not an edge. We do not report those.
- **Win rate is not the whole story.** The 0.25R target is what buys the high
  hit rate; the number that decides whether it makes money is the *expectancy*
  (+0.05R pooled, ~flat OOS). A 90% gate that loses money is not a win. The two
  must always be read together.

## What would actually get us to a validated 90%

The binding constraint is **sample depth**, and depth is blocked by data egress:

- IBKR MCP caps every pull at ~1000 bars ending *now*, so it cannot build the
  ~60-session-per-symbol history the walk-forward needs.
- Yahoo's v8 chart API *is* reachable here (query1/query2 return data), and a
  single clean pull gives ~60 sessions of 5-min and ~500 sessions of 1h per
  symbol — enough to roughly triple this study's sample. But Yahoo **rate-limits
  the shared egress IP (HTTP 429)** for anonymous requests, and the cookie/crumb
  hosts that would lift the limit (`fc.yahoo.com`, `finance.yahoo.com`) are
  proxy-blocked, so pulls only succeed intermittently. `scripts/fetch_yahoo_intraday.py`
  (with `--slow`) waits the limit out and resumes from disk; the pipeline
  (`sweeplib.data` SOURCES, `precision_filter.py` SERIES) is already wired to
  ingest `*_yf.json` the moment it lands. Running it from a **fresh session**,
  where the egress IP is not already throttled, is the reliable way to get the
  deep sample.
- With that sample, rerun `precision_filter.py` unchanged: if the ~85% IS gate
  holds on 3× the data with expectancy staying positive, the 90% claim becomes
  defensible; if it regresses to the low-80s, that is the honest ceiling of this
  edge and the target/gate should not be tortured further to manufacture 90%.
