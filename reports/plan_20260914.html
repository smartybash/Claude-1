What you are actually looking for

  1. Place all three as bracketed limit orders before the open. Then stop looking at them.
  2. One position at a time. When one fills, cancel the other two — the study only ever held one.
  3. If a fill happens, do nothing. The bracket takes it out at ±30 points. Median time in the trade was under three minutes.
  4. Never place an order at 29,415.00 or 29,384.88. Fading heavy prices lost 12 points a trade at a 33% win rate.
Why two prices are off limits. A level is only worth fading if the market rejected it last time. Heavy volume means the opposite — price was accepted there and settled in, so it slides back through rather than bouncing. 29,415.00 took 19,567 contracts on Friday. That is not support; that is where everyone agreed.
