## 1. RP-010 closure entry

**Recorded as instructed:**

- The final platform suite passed **115 assertions, zero failures**.
- The microsecond timestamp defect was corrected before the final result was
  interpreted.
- Initiative flow continued briefly for approximately one minute, then reversed.
- Absorption did not predict failure or reversal. It continued in the aggression
  direction.
- Absorption was materially asymmetric between buying and selling.
- Delta alone, price progress alone and volume alone each performed better than
  the combined impact state.
- Matched random times performed better than both treatment states.
- The best three sessions contributed **71.7%** of the only positive cell.
- Removing those sessions reduced the effect materially.
- **Eight of ten** kill conditions fired.
- **Nine of eleven** pass conditions failed.
- No entry, stop, target or strategy was defined.

**Verdict, as recorded:** the frozen order-flow impact construction is rejected.
Price progress relative to aggressive volume did not identify initiative
continuation or absorbed-flow reversal. Simpler variables and matched random
times performed better, and the remaining effect was concentrated in a few
sessions. **No Stage 2.**

**Qualification, as recorded.** The six-event cap bound on every session and
discarded 725 candidates. Because retention was chronological, the final sample
was the first six qualifying events of each session, not an unbiased sample
across the day. It produced exactly six events per session, no closing-period
events, and a first-come time-of-day bias. The precise conclusion is therefore:

> **The first six daily events generated by the frozen 30-second p90 aggression
> and p80/p20 impact construction have no tradeable predictive value.**

Not claimed: that all order flow is uninformative; that closing-period order flow
was tested; that every absorption definition has failed; that market impact never
matters.

### Research-integrity addition: capped event studies

Implemented as required. `dataquality.cap_diagnostics` returns, for any capped
event study:

| required figure | provided |
|---|---|
| % of sessions where the cap binds | `cap_binds_pct` |
| candidates discarded by the cap | `discarded`, `discarded_pct` |
| event distribution by time block before and after capping | `by_block_before`, `by_block_after` |
| whether any time block loses all observations | `blocks_emptied` |
| difference between retained and discarded candidates | the before/after block grids, which is where RP-010's difference lived |

`assert_cap_is_declared` **raises** when capping empties a time block, and
returns a mandatory `SAMPLING DESIGN:` sentence when the cap binds on more than
half of sessions. Platform test section 8 reproduces the RP-010 pattern on a
fixture — three sessions, four blocks, chronological retention — and asserts
that it raises and that a stratified rule does not. Ledger §12.

### The opening block cannot be filled, and the reason is a specification defect

| block | windows/session | median imbalance | block's own p90 | median volume | clears the pooled p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| opening | 50 | 0.0755 | 0.1678 | 1,038 | **3.5%** |
| morning | 180 | 0.0861 | 0.2091 | 626 | 7.2% |
| midday | 300 | 0.1091 | 0.2605 | 299 | 15.1% |
| closing | 230 | 0.1126 | 0.2670 | 270 | 16.5% |

The aggression gate is a percentile **pooled over the whole session**. Opening
windows carry 3.5× the midday volume and are the *least* one-sided, so the
opening block's own p90 imbalance (0.1678) sits **below** the pooled gate. Only
3.5% of opening windows clear it, against 16.5% at the close.

**This is RP-008's pooled-tercile defect in a new place: a threshold pooled
across the session partly encodes time of day — the very axis RP-011 sets out to
stratify on.** RP-011 freezes the RP-010 thresholds unchanged, so more data will
not fix it. At the observed rate the opening absorption cell needs ~250 sessions
(11.9 months) for 30 independent sessions, and the opening initiative cell was
**never once populated in 25 sessions**.

Flagged, not amended: changing the threshold to a block-relative percentile
would alter a frozen definition, which the brief forbids. It is your call
whether RP-011 becomes a six-cell study over three blocks, or whether the
threshold is re-specified before freezing.
