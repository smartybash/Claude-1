FOOTPRINT LEVELS — the reaction when price returns (61 discovery sessions)
==================================================================================================
  HEAVY   >= 2x the bar's median rung   (top decile)
  ORDINARY 0.8-1.2x        (the control)
  A return needs price to clear the level by 15 points
  first. Points are signed so positive means the level held, and
  are after 2 of cost.

  520 returns over 59 sessions

  median wait from level to return: 5 min

  Heavy levels beat zero. They do not beat the control. The first
  comparison is the one that flatters, and it is the wrong one.

==================================================================================================
PLACEBO: THE SAME TRADE AT A PRICE THAT WAS NEVER A LEVEL
==================================================================================================
  Identical machinery, identical horizons, identical signing. The
  only change is which price the trade waits for.

  price used                  n       +15m       +30m       +60m    t@15m
  the real level            520      -3.93      -4.29      -1.06    -1.27
  shifted +25 pts           511      -3.20      -7.55      -5.82    -1.16
  shifted -25 pts           486      +8.41      +9.13      +8.24    +3.07

  A price twenty-five points away from the level pays the same. The
  footprint level is decoration; the pullback is the entire effect,
  and it does not need a level, a footprint, or order flow to
  happen. That is the ninth finding to die here, and the first to be
  killed by a placebo rather than by a holdout.
