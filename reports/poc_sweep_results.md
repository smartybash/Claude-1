### What this shows

- POC-family sweep looks good on **NQ only** (+39.2 bp, p=0.065) and fails on the other 3. A one-instrument standout is exactly the overfitting trap this program pre-registers as NOT validated, regardless of how good the single cell looks.
- Scattered raw p<=0.05 cells with tiny n (e.g. round-number sweep on one instrument, n<=5) are multiplicity noise: this breakdown spans dozens of category x mode x K x instrument cells, so a few sub-0.05 p-values are expected by chance and none survive correction.

### Verdict

POC/value-area touches are handled by the same causal machinery as every other level and are subject to the same evidence bar: a real edge must be positive AND beat the matched null on >=3 of 4 instruments with multiplicity control, over enough sessions for a >=5-fold walk-forward. On the ~10-12 sessions obtainable here that bar cannot be cleared by POC or by any other level category - see the per-instrument tables. **NOT VALIDATED (insufficient depth).** The comparison above shows whether POC *looks* different from PDH/PDL in this sample; it is not evidence of an edge.
