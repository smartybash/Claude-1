# Conditioning existing trade sets on pre-open variables — declared before running

**No new rule is created and nothing is re-optimised.** Every trade below comes
from a rule already frozen in the ledger, regenerated byte-identically from the
existing modules. The only new thing is the *partition* of those trades by the
pre-open variables from `32620b8`.

**Sealed days stay sealed. 2016–2020 stays unread** — `QQQ_1m.parquet` spans
2021-01-04 → 2026-08-31 and cannot reach the holdout.

---

## 0. The state of the base sets, acknowledged before conditioning them

Every trade set below **already fails its own screen** under the strengthened
concentration rule adopted at `6c99ff1`:

| trade set | n | mean R | after max(10, ⌈0.10n⌉) | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IB 1R, single trade (the ex-frozen candidate) | 685 | +0.0612 | **−0.0433** | fails |
| IB 1R, with re-entry | 736 | +0.0628 | **−0.0412** | fails |
| IB 2R / 3R / 4R, with re-entry | 718–719 | +0.061 to +0.063 | −0.105 to −0.109 | fail |

| ORB-Fib continuation, ORB30 band A | 76 | +0.2071 | **−0.0616** | fails |

**This run therefore searches for a positive island inside a negative sea.**
That is a selection problem by construction: partitioning any negative set
finely enough will produce positive pieces. The random-label control is not a
formality here — it is the entire basis on which any subset could be believed.

## 3. The declared bar, and the arithmetic that decides what can be claimed

A subset claiming to be tradeable must meet **all four**:

1. **n ≥ 150 trades**
2. **mean R > 0** after costs
3. **positive after removing max(10, ⌈0.10 n⌉) trades** — the standing rule
4. **≥ 4 of 6 years positive** within the subset

### 3.1 The concentration rule gets harsher as subsets shrink — stated now

| subset n | trades removed | fraction removed |
|---|---|---|
| 685 (full IB set) | 69 | 10.0% |
| 342 (half) | 35 | 10.2% |
| 171 (quartile) | 18 | 10.5% |
| 150 (the floor) | 15 | 10.0% |
| 112 (ORB15 A, whole set) | 12 | 10.7% |
| 68 (a decile of the IB set) | 10 | **14.7%** |
| 76 (ORB30 A, whole set) | 10 | **13.2%** |

Below n = 100 the `max(10, ·)` arm binds and the rule removes **more than a
tenth**. This is declared rather than discovered: a small subset is held to a
strictly harder test than the full set it came from.

The base set's top decile carries **164% of its total R**. A subset survives
rule 3 only if its edge is *less* tail-concentrated than its parent's — which
is the opposite of what quantile-slicing a fat-tailed set tends to produce.

### 3.2 Which subsets can clear the 150-trade floor at all

This is the key arithmetic and it is stated before any result.

| partition | IB sets (n≈685–736) | ORB-Fib (n=112, 76) |
|---|---|---|
| **decile** (n≈68–74) | **cannot reach 150** | cannot |
| quintile (n≈137–147) | **cannot reach 150** | cannot |
| quartile (n≈171–184) | can | cannot |
| half (n≈343–368) | can | cannot |
| FOMC = 1 (≈24 trades) | **cannot reach 150** | cannot |
| FOMC = 0 (≈661 trades) | can (≈ the whole set) | cannot |

**Three consequences, declared now:**

- **No decile can ever be a tradeable claim.** Deciles are reported because
  they were asked for and because the *shape* of the ladder is informative, but
  a decile of a 685-trade set is 68 trades and fails rule 1 by construction.
- **Neither ORB-Fib set can be resolved at all.** The whole variant is 112 and
  76 trades; every subset is smaller. Like the ORB-Fib reversal family, this is
  recorded as **not resolvable on available data**, not as a failure.
- **No FOMC-day subset can be claimed.** ~48 FOMC dates yield ~24 IB trades.

**Claimable subsets:** 5 continuous conditions × 4 partitions (top half, bottom
half, top quartile, bottom quartile) × 5 IB sets = **100**, plus 5 FOMC-negative
subsets = **105**. The VIX conditions cover 1,229 of 1,395 sessions, so their
quartiles sit near n ≈ 151 — barely over the floor, and flagged when they land
below it.

**Total subsets examined, including the ones that cannot be claimed:**
7 sets × (5 conditions × 4 partitions + 2 FOMC groups) = **154**.
Bonferroni over 154 would be α = 0.000325; the max-statistic control below is
used instead because these subsets overlap heavily and Bonferroni would be
both wrong and needlessly conservative.

### 3.3 Minimum detectable effect, before results

Per-trade sd is ≈ 0.72 R. With clustered errors by date and ≈1 trade per date
in the single-trade set:

| subset n | SE of mean R | two-sided 95% MDE |
|---|---|---|
| 685 | 0.028 | ±0.054 |
| 342 | 0.039 | ±0.076 |
| 171 | 0.055 | **±0.108** |
| 150 | 0.059 | **±0.115** |

**A quartile subset must show mean R ≈ +0.11 to be distinguishable from zero.**
The parent set's whole effect is +0.061. So any subset that passes must be
roughly **twice as strong as the parent** — and must be so while surviving a
concentration rule that removes 10.5% of its trades.

## 4. The random-label control, which governs

**Design.** Condition rows are permuted **across dates**, keeping the six
conditions together so their mutual correlation structure is preserved. Trades
inherit their (permuted) date's conditions. Subsets are then cut as trade-space
quantiles, so **group sizes are matched exactly by construction** — a top
quartile is always ⌈n/4⌉ trades, permuted or not.

**Statistic.** The date-clustered t-statistic of mean R, so subsets of different
sizes are comparable.

**Bar.** 5,000 permutations. For each, the **maximum** t over all subsets is
recorded. The observed best subset must exceed the **95th percentile of that
maximum distribution**. This prices the search over 154 overlapping subsets
correctly, which a per-subset p value would not.

Both a per-trade-set max and a **global max across all 154 subsets** are
reported. **The global one governs the decision.**

---

## 5. The decision, declared

> **If no subset beats the 95th percentile of the random-label maximum, the
> direction closes.**

A subset that beats the control still has to pass all four bars in §3 before it
is called tradeable. Beating the control alone is necessary, not sufficient.

**No new hypotheses.** If this closes, conditioning is finished as a direction
and nothing further is proposed in it.
